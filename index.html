THE REQUIRED CHECK / BFSK

Are the tones
orthogonal?

For noncoherent detection, each tone occupies a two-dimensional sine/cosine signal space. Orthogonal spaces require tone spacing of an integer multiple of 1 / Tb; the minimum is 1 / Tb. Coherent BFSK can use a minimum spacing of 1 / (2Tb) when the carrier phases are known and chosen for orthogonality.
